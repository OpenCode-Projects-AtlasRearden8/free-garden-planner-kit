Planting Calendar by Season

A friendly getting-started guide

Gardening isn't one big spring event — it's four small seasons. This calendar gives you a place to plan what happens when, from the first cool-season crops to the final fall cleanup.

How to use it

  1. Start with your frost dates from the Seed Starting Log. Everything hinges on them.
  2. Spring is for cool-season crops: peas, lettuce, spinach, radishes, carrots, broccoli. These go in 2–4 weeks before your last frost.
  3. Summer is for the heat lovers: tomatoes, peppers, beans, cucumbers, squash — planted after frost danger passes.
  4. Fall gets a second round of the cool crops, plus garlic. Count backwards from your first frost date.
  5. Winter is for planning, ordering seeds, and resting. You've earned it.
Beginner tip: Write in pencil or expect to scribble. Weather moves dates every year, and that's normal. The calendar is a plan, not a contract.
