End-of-Season Reflections

A friendly getting-started guide

The last page of the season is the most valuable one. Ten minutes of honest notes now will make next spring's plan dramatically better — and future you will be grateful someone wrote this down.

How to use it

  1. Do it before you forget. The best time is right after final cleanup, while the season is still fresh.
  2. Be specific. “Cherry tomatoes did great” is nice. “Sungold, north bed, produced until frost” is useful.
  3. Include the failures kindly. A crop that flopped isn't a mistake — it's data. Wrong spot, wrong season, wrong variety: name it and move on.
  4. Answer the big question: what would make next year easier or more fun? That's your first note for next season's plan.
Beginner tip: File this page with your seed inventory and next year's calendar. Together they become your garden's yearbook.
