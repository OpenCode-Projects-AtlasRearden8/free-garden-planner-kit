Watering Tracker

A friendly getting-started guide

Most beginner garden problems trace back to water: too much, too little, or all-at-once-then-forgotten. This tracker helps you build a steady rhythm and notice what your garden actually needs.

How to use it

  1. List your beds or containers down the left column — one row each, not one per plant.
  2. Check a box each day you water that bed. An empty week of boxes is information too: maybe that bed didn't need it, or maybe it was forgotten.
  3. Use the rain row. Mark days it rained hard enough to soak the soil — roughly a half inch or more. Rain counts as watering.
  4. Test before you pour. Stick a finger two inches into the soil. Dry there? Water deeply. Damp? Skip and check tomorrow.
Beginner tip: Water deeply and less often instead of a little splash every day. Deep watering encourages roots to grow down, where they survive hot spells.
When to water: Early morning is ideal. Leaves have time to dry, and less water evaporates in the midday sun.
